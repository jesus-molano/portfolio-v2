STATS map and portrait (Vice Afterglow)

map.svg, map-square.svg
  Drawn by tools/art/stats/map.mjs for this site. The coast, relief and
  depth of Tenerife and Gran Canaria come from tools/art/stats/canaries.json,
  extracted by tools/art/stats/extract.mjs from the Terrain Tiles on AWS
  Open Data (https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/), zoom 8, which at
  these islands are built only from:
    - SRTM (NASA / USGS), public domain
    - GMTED2010 (USGS), public domain
    - ETOPO1 (NOAA), public domain
  Courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey, NASA and NOAA. Roads are schematic
  curves through the real towns, not traced from any map. Place names on
  the page are parodies written for this site.
