Modified for this site with Blender (tools/blender/process_convertible.py):
converted from OBJ to glTF (GLB), wheels split into separate nodes, front
seat backrests lowered and both front seats moved 0.2 m forward. At runtime
the materials are replaced with opaque paint (the source declares glass).

The site shows the credit, with links to the source and the licence and a
note that the model is modified, alongside the other asset attributions.
