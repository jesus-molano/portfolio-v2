The driver: built with MakeHuman / MPFB 2 (https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb.html)
from the MakeHuman system assets.

License of the assets used:
CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0) Public Domain Dedication
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The MakeHuman base mesh, targets, rig, skin, eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes and
the male_casualsuit06 clothes were explicitly released as CC0 in September
2020 (see the license notes in each asset file). No community asset is used.

Made for this site (tools/blender/build_driver_mpfb.py): face shape, smirk,
hair with a skin fade, beard and moustache (geometry shells and baked
textures), striped tee with a burgundy collar and a black earring. Rigged
with MPFB's built-in game_engine rig (part of the CC0 assets above) and
exported from Blender as glTF with WebP textures.
